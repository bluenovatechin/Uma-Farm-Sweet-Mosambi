Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Last Updated: October 12, 2026 Location: Shakti Nagar, Sabarkantha, Gujarat

At Uma Farm Sweet Mosambi ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your details when you browse our website, submit a wholesale inquiry, or contact us via phone or WhatsApp.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect details directly submitted by you for wholesale trade and agricultural order coordination:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone/WhatsApp number, and delivery destination city.
  • Commercial Details: Business name (if applicable), buyer category (mandi agent, juice chain, fruit vendor), and requested fruit volume.
  • Delivery Notes: Preferred harvest batches, crate requirements, or farm-gate pickup schedules.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used strictly for authentic agricultural business interactions:

  • To verify harvest volume, calculate crate pricing, and schedule dispatches from our Sabarkantha orchard.
  • To connect with you directly on phone or WhatsApp (+91 94260 40220).
  • To coordinate mandi logistics and truck transport to your city.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact records with third-party marketing brokers. Details are shared strictly with transport drivers or freight handlers to deliver your crates, or as required by Indian legal authorities.

4. Direct WhatsApp Inquiries

When you use our wholesale inquiry form, the website formats your message and launches WhatsApp directly to our official farm number (+91 94260 40220). No sensitive payment details or backend databases store personal passwords on this site.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential local storage strictly to remember your cookie preference banner so it does not interrupt your browsing.
