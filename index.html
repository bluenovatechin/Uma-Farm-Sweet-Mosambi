About Uma Farm

A family-run orchard dedicated to pure sweet lime cultivation.

Located in the fertile farming soil of Shakti Nagar near Gujarat State Highway in Sabarkantha district, Uma Farm specializes strictly in sweet lime (mosambi) fruit production.

We believe in honest, authentic farming: no harmful chemical carbide accelerants, no forced artificial ripening, and no compromised grades. Every tree is spaced 20×20 feet to capture balanced Gujarat sunshine and watered through direct drip lines.

Orchard Management

Scientific care from root to canopy.

  1. 20×20 Feet Tree Spacing

    Every sweet lime tree enjoys dedicated ground space, allowing full 360-degree sunlight penetration and cross airflow that prevents fungal buildup.

  2. Root-Zone Drip Lines

    Deep water lines channel irrigation directly to roots, reducing evaporation and keeping foliage dry and disease-free.

  3. Regular Pruning & Desuckering

    Water shoots and non-productive growth are trimmed each season so nutrients feed the main fruit-bearing canopies.

  4. Harvested with Clean Tools

    Fruits are clipped carefully at the stem without tearing the rind, maximizing crate shelf life during mandi transit.

How is the wholesale price of mosambi determined?

Our prices are set per kilogram or per crate according to natural grading, harvest volume, and prevailing APMC mandi market rates in Gujarat. Direct orchard buyers save intermediate commission costs.

Are the mosambi fruits 100% naturally grown?

Yes. We use deep drip irrigation systems and allow fruits to naturally ripen under the Sabarkantha sun on tree canopies. We do not use harmful chemical accelerators or artificial carbide.

Can juice centers and fruit vendors purchase small crates?

Yes. We welcome commercial juice chains, local fruit vendors, and bulk visitors for direct farm-gate collection in Sabarkantha as well as multi-crate consignments.

How does Uma Farm pack sweet limes for transport?

Fruits are harvested at dawn to lock in coolness and moisture, graded for skin quality, and packed carefully into ventilated crates to prevent road transit damage.
