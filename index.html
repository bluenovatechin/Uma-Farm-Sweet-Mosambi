About the farm

A family orchard that grows only mosambi

Our farm is at Adalpura in Dhansura taluka, Aravalli district. We grow one crop, sweet mosambi, and look after every tree ourselves.

Trees are planted 20 by 20 feet apart so each one gets full sun and air. Every tree is watered by drip line, pruned each season and harvested by hand.

FAQ

Common questions

Where is Uma Farm located?

Uma Farm is at Adalpura, Dhansura, Aravalli district, Gujarat 383310. Use our Google Maps location for directions to the farm.

How is the wholesale price of mosambi decided?

We quote per crate or per kg. The rate depends on fruit grade and size, the quantity and the current APMC mandi rate in Gujarat, so it changes with the market. Ask us on WhatsApp for today's rate.

Is your mosambi naturally ripened?

Yes. The fruit ripens on the tree. We do not use calcium carbide or any chemical to ripen or colour the fruit.
